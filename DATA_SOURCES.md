## BOE documents

The legal documents under `docs/` were obtained from the Agencia Estatal
Boletin Oficial del Estado (AEBOE). Source: https://www.boe.es.

The AEBOE legal notice states that works in its Biblioteca Juridica Digital are
normally distributed under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 unless a work says otherwise. Its
reuse conditions also require attribution, preservation of update and reuse
metadata, and no suggestion of official status, participation, sponsorship, or
endorsement. Review the current terms before redistributing a document:

https://www.boe.es/informacion/aviso_legal/index.php

Only the electronic texts published by the official gazette have official and
authentic status. Material in this repository is supplied for research and
demonstration and is not legal advice.

## Synthetic HR data

The CSV files under `data/` are synthetic demonstration fixtures. They must not
be treated as real employment or medical records. Contributors must never commit
real personal, employment, payroll, health, or absence data.
