# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give others credit.
- Protect private information and report security concerns responsibly.
- Prefer language and decisions that make participation easier for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the primary maintainer through the contact
method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and in
good faith. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when needed
to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository activity and to public spaces where someone is
representing the project.
